UNIT 9 LESSON PLAN

Equations of Lines and Planes

Course: MCV4U, Grade 12 Calculus and Vectors  |  Unit: 9 — Equations of Lines and Planes (Nelson Ch. 8–9)  |  Time: 12–14 periods (~75 min each)

Curriculum expectations (Strand C — Geometry and Algebra of Vectors)

  • C1.1 — Determine vector and parametric equations of a line in R2; connect Cartesian, vector, and parametric forms (8.1, 8.2).
  • C1.2 — Determine vector, parametric, and symmetric equations of a line in R3 (8.3).
  • C1.3 — Determine vector, parametric, and Cartesian equations of a plane; determine geometric properties; convert between forms; sketch planes in R3 (8.4–8.6).
  • C1.4 — Determine intersections: line–plane, two lines (incl. skew), two planes, three planes; solve 2x2 and 3x3 systems by elimination (9.1–9.4).
  • C1.5 — Determine distances: point–line (R2, R3), point–plane, skew lines; interpret geometrically (9.5, 9.6).

Assessment plan

  • For learning: Entry Card, Formative Checks A/B/C with answer-specific feedback.
  • As learning: Exit Card, Unit 9 Discussion.
  • Of learning: Summative Assignment (16 Q, 100 pts), Summative Test (26 Q, 100 pts, 120 min).

Lesson sequence

  1. Entry Card → Lesson 9.1 (vector/parametric lines in R2) → Lesson 9.2 (Cartesian lines in R2) → Formative Check A.
  2. Lesson 9.3 (lines in R3: vector, parametric, symmetric) → Lesson 9.4 (planes: vector, parametric, Cartesian; sketching) → Formative Check B.
  3. Lesson 9.5 (intersections: line–plane, two lines; systems of equations) → Lesson 9.6 (intersections of planes; distances) → Formative Check C.
  4. Summative Assignment → Summative Test → Discussion → Exit Card.

Materials

Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12 Ch. 8–9; Desmos (embedded in each lesson); curated video per lesson; original concept diagrams embedded in lessons.
