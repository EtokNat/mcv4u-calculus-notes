UNIT 9 OVERVIEW

Equations of Lines and Planes

Textbook: Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12, Chapters 8 (pp. 424–485) and 9 (pp. 486–556). Strand C — Geometry and Algebra of Vectors. Suggested pacing: 12–14 periods.

Unit 8 gave you vectors as algebraic objects. This unit turns them into geometry: one point plus one direction vector describes every line in R2 and R3; one point plus two direction vectors (or one normal vector) describes every plane in R3. Then the geometry becomes algebra again — finding where lines and planes meet means solving systems of equations, and the shortest distances come from projections onto normals and direction vectors. The unit closes with the engineer's question from the Chapter 9 Career Link: how far apart are two skew pipelines?

Ontario curriculum expectations (Chapters 8–9)

  • Determine the vector and parametric equations of a line in two-space (8.1).
  • Make connections between Cartesian, vector, and parametric equations of a line in two-space (8.2).
  • Determine the vector, parametric, and symmetric equations of a line in 3-space (8.3).
  • Determine the vector, parametric, and Cartesian equations of a plane; determine geometric properties of a plane; convert between forms (8.4, 8.5).
  • Sketch a plane in 3-space (8.6).
  • Determine the intersection between a line and a plane and between two lines in three-dimensional space (9.1).
  • Algebraically solve systems of equations involving up to three equations in three unknowns (9.2).
  • Determine the intersection of two or three planes (9.3, 9.4).
  • Determine the distance from a point to a line in two- and three-dimensional space (9.5).
  • Determine the distance from a point to a plane; solve distance problems relating to lines and planes and interpret the results geometrically (9.6).

Learning Goals

  • I can write vector, parametric, and symmetric equations of lines in R2 and R3, and test points and convert between forms.
  • I can write vector, parametric, and Cartesian equations of planes in R3, find normals via the cross product, and sketch planes.
  • I can find intersections of lines with planes, pairs of lines (including skew lines), pairs of planes, and triples of planes.
  • I can solve 2x2 and 3x3 systems of equations by elimination and connect the algebra to the geometry.
  • I can compute distances from a point to a line (R2 and R3), from a point to a plane, and between skew lines.
