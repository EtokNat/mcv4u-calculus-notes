UNIT 8 OVERVIEW

Applications of Vectors

Textbook: Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12, Chapter 7 "Applications of Vectors" (applied material from §§7.1, 7.2, 7.7). Strand C — Geometry and Algebra of Vectors. Suggested pacing: 10–12 periods.

Unit 7 gave you the vector toolkit — dot products, projections, and cross products. This unit takes those tools into the real world. You will combine forces into resultants and balance them into equilibrium, correct headings for wind and current, compute the work done by angled pulls and the torque of wrenches and doors, resolve vectors into useful components on ramps and runways, and measure distances, areas, and volumes straight from coordinates. Every lesson is a word problem you could meet outside the classroom.

Ontario curriculum expectations (Chapter 7, applied)

  • Solve problems involving forces in equilibrium and resultant forces in two dimensions (7.1).
  • Solve problems involving relative velocity and navigation, including wind and current corrections (7.2).
  • Apply the dot product to solve problems involving work (7.7; computation in Unit 7, Lesson 7.6).
  • Apply the cross product to solve problems involving torque (7.7; computation in Unit 7, Lesson 7.6).
  • Solve problems involving the projection of one vector onto another in applied contexts (7.5; computation in Unit 7, Lesson 7.3).
  • Solve geometric problems involving distances, areas of parallelograms and triangles, and volumes using vectors (7.7; areas in Unit 7, Lesson 7.5).

Learning Goals

  • I can find the resultant and equilibrant of real force systems and solve static-equilibrium problems (hanging masses, ramps, cables).
  • I can combine vehicle velocity with wind/current to find ground velocity, and determine headings that hold a desired course.
  • I can compute work done by angled forces, combine the work of multiple forces, and interpret negative work.
  • I can compute torque for tools and mechanisms and solve rotational-balance problems.
  • I can resolve a vector into components along and perpendicular to a direction of interest (ramps, ropes, headings).
  • I can find distances, land areas, and cargo volumes from vector coordinates.

How you will be assessed

  • Entry Card — short survey before the unit (assessment for learning).
  • Exit Card — short self-assessment survey after the unit (assessment as learning).
  • Unit 8 Discussion — Resultant or Torque: which one explains it? (assessment as learning).
  • Formative Checks A, B, C — practice quizzes with feedback on every answer (for learning, 0 points).
  • Unit 8 Summative Assignment — 16 questions, 100 points (of learning).
  • Unit 8 Summative Test — 26 questions, 100 points, 120-minute limit (of learning).
