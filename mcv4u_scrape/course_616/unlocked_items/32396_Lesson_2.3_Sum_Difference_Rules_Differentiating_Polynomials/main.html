💡 Minds On

📖 Learn — step by step

The Sum Rule. If p(x) and q(x) are differentiable and f(x) = p(x) + q(x), then f′(x) = p′(x) + q′(x). In Leibniz notation, d/dx[f(x)] = d/dx[p(x)] + d/dx[q(x)].
Proof: from the definition, f′(x) = limh→0 ([p(x+h) + q(x+h)] − [p(x) + q(x)])/h; regrouping gives the two difference quotients, whose limits are p′(x) and q′(x).
The Difference Rule. If p(x) and q(x) are differentiable and f(x) = p(x) − q(x), then f′(x) = p′(x) − q′(x). (The proof is similar to the sum rule.)

Example 4 — Differentiate:
a. f(x) = 3x² − 5√x   b. y = (3x + 2)²

Solution. Apply the constant multiple, power, sum, and difference rules.
a. f′(x) = d/dx(3x²) − d/dx(5x^(1/2)) = 3·d/dx(x²) − 5·d/dx(x^(1/2)) = 3(2x) − 5((1/2)x^(−1/2)) = 6x − 5/(2√x).
b. First expand y = (3x + 2)² = 9x² + 12x + 4. Then dy/dx = 9(2x) + 12(1) + 0 = 18x + 12.

Example 5 — Determine the equation of the tangent to the graph of f(x) = −x³ + 3x² − 2 at x = 1.

Solution. The slope of the tangent at any point is given by f′(x). For f(x) = −x³ + 3x² − 2:
f′(x) = −3x² + 6x. Now, f′(1) = −3(1)² + 6(1) = −3 + 6 = 3.
The slope at x = 1 is 3 and the point of tangency is (1, f(1)) = (1, 0). The tangent is y − 0 = 3(x − 1), or y = 3x − 3.

Example 6 — Determine points on the graph in Example 5 where the tangents are horizontal.

Solution. Horizontal lines have slope zero, so solve f′(x) = 0:
−3x² + 6x = 0 → −3x(x − 2) = 0 → x = 0 or x = 2.
The graph of f(x) = −x³ + 3x² − 2 has horizontal tangents at (0, −2) and (2, 2).

✅ Check Your Understanding

Try these on paper first, then reveal. Modelled on Exercise 2.2.

Question 1 — Find f′(x) for f(x) = 4x² − 7x + 8.

Term by term: 8x − 7.

Question 2 — Find the tangent slope to y = 2x³ + 3x at x = 1.

y′ = 6x² + 3; at x = 1 the slope is 9.

Question 3 — Find the point(s) with a horizontal tangent on y = 2x − 5x².

y′ = 2 − 10x = 0 → x = 1/5; y = 1/5. Point: (1/5, 1/5).

Question 4 — Find the equation of the tangent to y = 2x − 1/x at the point (0.5, −1).

Rewrite as 2x − x⁻¹: y′ = 2 + x⁻²; at x = 0.5, slope = 2 + 4 = 6. Tangent: y + 1 = 6(x − 0.5), or y = 6x − 4.

📝 Lesson Summary

Key ideas

  • Sum rule: (p + q)′ = p′ + q′. Difference rule: (p − q)′ = p′ − q′.
  • With the five rules (constant, power, constant multiple, sum, difference) you can differentiate any polynomial.

Need to know

  • Sometimes expanding first is easier — e.g., (3x + 2)².
  • Rewrite roots and reciprocals as powers before differentiating.
  • Horizontal tangents occur where f′(x) = 0.
