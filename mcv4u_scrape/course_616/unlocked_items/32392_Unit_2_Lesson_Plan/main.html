Unit 2 Lesson Plan — Derivatives

Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12, Chapter 2 — for teacher reference.

Course: MCV4U, Grade 12 Calculus and Vectors  |  Unit: 2 — Derivatives (Nelson Ch. 2)  |  Time: 12–14 periods (~75 min each)

Curriculum expectations

  • §2.1 — Determine derivatives of polynomial and simple rational functions from first principles; identify non-differentiable functions.
  • §2.2 — Justify and use the constant, power, constant multiple, sum, and difference rules.
  • §2.3 — Justify and use the product rule (including the power-of-a-function form).
  • §2.4 — Justify and use the quotient rule.
  • §2.5 — Decompose composite functions; justify and use the chain rule.
  • §2.2–§2.5 — Solve problems involving instantaneous rates of change (tangents, normals, motion, economics).

Learning goals

Students will construct the derivative function from the limit definition and recognize where derivatives fail; differentiate efficiently with the full rule set (choosing correctly among product, quotient, and chain rules); find tangent and normal equations; and apply derivatives to instantaneous rates of change, including the chapter's elasticity-of-demand investigation.

Success criteria

  • I can state which rule I am using at each step and why that rule fits the function's structure.
  • I can rewrite roots, reciprocals, and negative exponents as powers before differentiating.
  • I can explain why (fg)′ ≠ f′g′ and why the chain rule needs the inner derivative as a factor.
  • I can attach correct units and meaning to every rate of change I compute.

Assessment plan

  • For learning: Entry Card (feelings/goals), Formative Checks A/B/C with answer-specific feedback.
  • As learning: Exit Card (self-assessment), Unit 2 Discussion (elasticity — peer critique of reasoning).
  • Of learning: Summative Assignment (16 Q, 100 pts, hybrid auto/teacher-marked), Summative Test (26 Q, 100 pts, 120 min, hybrid).

Lesson sequence

  1. Entry Card → Lesson 2.1 (derivative function, first principles, non-differentiable cases).
  2. Lesson 2.2 (constant, power, constant multiple rules; rewriting as powers) → Formative Check A.
  3. Lesson 2.3 (sum/difference rules; tangents, normals, horizontal tangents) → Lesson 2.4 (product rule).
  4. Lesson 2.5 (quotient rule; two routes for rational functions) → Formative Check B.
  5. Lesson 2.6 (chain rule; combining rules) → Formative Check C.
  6. Chapter Task investigation (elasticity of demand, p. 108) → Discussion → Summative Assignment → Summative Test → Exit Card.

Materials

Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12 Ch. 2; Desmos (embedded in each lesson); curated video per lesson; textbook figures embedded in lessons and two quiz stems.

Differentiation

Formative checks give instant targeted feedback for self-pacing; every MC option explains the specific error it represents. Extension: textbook "Create" problems (e.g. 2.3 Q11–Q14, 2.5 Q17–Q18) and the Chapter Task parts (b)–(d). Support: Desmos explorations verify each rule numerically before the algebra, and the Lesson 2.3 video may be assigned in segments.
