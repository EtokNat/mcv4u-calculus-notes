UNIT 7 LESSON PLAN

Dot Product and Cross Product

Course: MCV4U, Grade 12 Calculus and Vectors  |  Unit: 7 — Dot Product and Cross Product (Nelson Ch. 7, §§7.3–7.7)  |  Time: 10–12 periods (~75 min each)

Curriculum expectations (Strand C — Geometry and Algebra of Vectors)

  • C1.1 — Perform the dot product on geometric and algebraic vectors: a·b = |a||b|cosθ and a·b = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃ (7.3, 7.4).
  • C1.2 — Determine properties of the dot product: commutative, distributive, magnitudes property, scalar-factor rule; perpendicularity test a·b = 0 (7.3, 7.4).
  • C1.3 — Determine scalar and vector projections of a vector; decompose a vector into parallel and perpendicular components (7.5).
  • C1.4 — Perform the cross product on algebraic vectors in R³: a × b = |a||b|sinθ·n̂, right-hand rule, component formula (7.6).
  • C1.5 — Determine properties of the cross product: anti-commutative, distributive, a × a = 0, unit-vector products; parallel test a × b = 0 (7.6).
  • C1.6 — Solve problems involving dot and cross products: angles, work, torque, areas of parallelograms and triangles (7.7).

🎯 Learning Goals

Students will compute dot products geometrically and algebraically; find angles between vectors and classify them; test perpendicularity and parallelism; compute scalar and vector projections and decompose vectors; compute cross products geometrically (right-hand rule) and from components; apply |a × b| to areas; and solve work, torque, and mixed application problems, choosing the correct product for each situation.

Success criteria

  • I can compute a·b from magnitudes + angle and from components, and explain when each form is the right tool.
  • I can find θ = cos⁻¹((a·b)/(|a||b|)) and state whether it is acute, right, or obtuse.
  • I can use a·b = 0 (perpendicular) and a × b = 0 (parallel) as zero tests, for nonzero vectors.
  • I can find projb a = ((a·b)/|b|²) b and verify a − projb a ⊥ b.
  • I can compute a × b in R³, apply the right-hand rule, and never swap the order carelessly (a × b = −b × a).
  • I can get a parallelogram area as |a × b|, a triangle area as half of that, work as F·d, and torque as r × F.

Assessment plan

  • For learning: Entry Card (prior knowledge of vectors, confidence, goals), Formative Checks A/B/C with answer-specific feedback.
  • As learning: Exit Card (self-assessment against entry goals), Unit 7 Discussion (peer explanation: "when do you dot and when do you cross?").
  • Of learning: Summative Assignment (16 Q, 100 pts, hybrid auto/teacher-marked), Summative Test (26 Q, 100 pts, 120 min, hybrid).

Lesson sequence

  1. Entry Card → Lesson 7.1 (dot product: geometric definition) — the scalar product, sign, perpendicularity test.
  2. Lesson 7.2 (dot product: algebraic form) — component formula, angle from coordinates → Formative Check A (7.1 + 7.2).
  3. Lesson 7.3 (angles and projections) — the angle formula, scalar/vector projections, vector decomposition.
  4. Lesson 7.4 (cross product: definition) — |a||b|sinθ, right-hand rule, R³ component formula → Formative Check B (7.3 + 7.4).
  5. Lesson 7.5 (cross product: properties and area) — parallelogram/triangle areas, i/j/k table, zero tests.
  6. Lesson 7.6 (mixed applications) — work, torque, area, choosing the product → Formative Check C (7.5 + 7.6, mixed).
  7. Summative Assignment → Summative Test → Discussion → Exit Card.

Materials

Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12 Ch. 7; Desmos (embedded in each lesson); curated Khan Academy video per lesson; original vector diagrams embedded in lessons.

Differentiation

Formative checks give instant targeted feedback for self-pacing. Extension: proof-style problems (e.g. the parallelogram identity |u+v|² + |u−v|² = 2|u|² + 2|v|², Gram–Schmidt-style projection chains, the sinθ proof from the dot product) and the triple-product identity. Support: Desmos explorations make the sign of the dot product and the shadow of a projection visual before the algebra; the decision guide in Lesson 7.6 gives a repeatable scaffold ("number → dot; perpendicular vector or area → cross").
