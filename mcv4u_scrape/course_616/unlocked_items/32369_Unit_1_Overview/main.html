UNIT 1 OVERVIEW

Introduction to Calculus

Textbook: Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12, Chapter 1 (pp. 4–60). Strand A — Rate of Change. Suggested pacing: 10–12 periods.

Calculus was built to answer two questions: how steep is a curve at a single point, and how much area sits under it? This unit builds the machinery — secants, tangents, rates of change, and limits — that makes those answers possible.

Ontario curriculum expectations (Chapter 1)

  • Simplify radical expressions (1.1).
  • Use limits to determine the slope and the equation of the tangent to a graph (1.2).
  • Pose problems and formulate hypotheses regarding rates of change; calculate and interpret average and instantaneous rates of change and relate them to slopes of secants and tangents (1.3).
  • Understand and evaluate limits using appropriate properties (1.4, 1.5).
  • Examine continuous functions and use limits to explain why a function is discontinuous (1.5, 1.6).

🎯 Learning Goals

  • I can rationalize denominators and numerators with conjugates.
  • I can estimate and compute the slope of a tangent as the limit of secant slopes.
  • I can calculate average and instantaneous rates of change and interpret them in context.
  • I can evaluate limits graphically, numerically, and algebraically, including one-sided limits.
  • I can test continuity with the three conditions and classify discontinuities.

How you will be assessed

  • Exit Card — short self-assessment survey after the unit (assessment as learning).
  • Unit 1 Discussion — Why 0/0 Is Not an Answer (assessment as learning).

  • Unit 1 Summative Assignment — 16 questions, 100 points (of learning).
  • Unit 1 Summative Test — 26 questions, 100 points, 120-minute limit (of learning).
