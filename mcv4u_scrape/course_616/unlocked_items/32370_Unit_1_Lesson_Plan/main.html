UNIT 1 LESSON PLAN

Introduction to Calculus

Course: MCV4U, Grade 12 Calculus and Vectors  |  Unit: 1 — Introduction to Calculus (Nelson Ch. 1)  |  Time: 10–12 periods (~75 min each)

Curriculum expectations (Strand A — Rate of Change)

  • A1.1 — Simplify radical expressions.
  • A1.2 — Determine the slope of a tangent and its equation using limits.
  • A1.3 — Calculate and interpret average and instantaneous rates of change; connect them to secant and tangent slopes.
  • A1.4 — Evaluate limits using limit properties, including one-sided limits.
  • A1.5 — Determine continuity at a point and classify discontinuities using limits.

🎯 Learning Goals

Students will rationalize radical expressions; estimate tangent slopes from secants and compute them with the difference quotient; distinguish average from instantaneous rate of change in applied contexts; evaluate limits by graphing, tables, and algebra; and justify continuity with the three-condition test.

Success criteria

  • I can show each algebraic step when rationalizing or evaluating a limit.
  • I can explain why the tangent slope is a limit of secant slopes, not a single calculation.
  • I can attach correct units and a real-world meaning to every rate of change.
  • I can state which continuity condition fails when a function is discontinuous.

Assessment plan

  • For learning: Entry Card (prior knowledge), Formative Checks A/B/C with answer-specific feedback.
  • As learning: Exit Card (self-assessment), Unit 1 Discussion (peer explanation).
  • Of learning: Summative Assignment (16 Q, 100 pts, hybrid auto/teacher-marked), Summative Test (26 Q, 100 pts, 120 min, hybrid).

Lesson sequence

  1. Entry Card → Lesson 1.1 (rationalizing) — algebra prerequisite.
  2. Lesson 1.2 (tangent slope via secants) + Desmos secant explorer → Formative Check A.
  3. Lesson 1.3 (rates of change) with applied contexts.
  4. Lesson 1.4 (limits, one-sided) → Formative Check B.
  5. Lesson 1.5 (limit properties, 0/0 techniques).
  6. Lesson 1.6 (continuity, discontinuity types) → Formative Check C.
  7. Chapter Task investigation → Summative Assignment → Summative Test → Exit Card.

Materials

Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12 Ch. 1; Desmos (embedded in each lesson); curated video per lesson; textbook figures embedded in lessons.

Differentiation

Formative checks give instant targeted feedback for self-pacing. Extension: textbook "Create" problems (e.g. 1.3 Q18–Q22, 1.5 Q14–Q17). Support: Desmos explorations make the limiting process visual before the algebra.
