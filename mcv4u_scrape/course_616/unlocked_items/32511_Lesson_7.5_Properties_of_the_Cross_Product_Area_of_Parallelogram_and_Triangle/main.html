🎯 Learning Goals

By the end of this lesson, you can:

  • Find the area of a parallelogram as |a × b| and of a triangle as |a × b|/2.
  • Apply the cross-product properties: anti-commutative, distributive, scalar factor, and a × a = 0.
  • Use the i, j, k multiplication table (i × j = k, j × k = i, k × i = j).
  • Recognize that a zero cross product means the vectors are parallel.

📖 Learn — step by step

Area from the cross product

Take the parallelogram with adjacent sides a and b. Its base is |a| and its height is |b|sinθ (the opposite side of the right triangle with hypotenuse |b|). So:

Area of parallelogram = |a||b|sinθ = |a × b|
Area of triangle = |a × b| / 2

The triangle result follows because a diagonal cuts any parallelogram into two congruent triangles. No coordinates are needed — any pair of edge vectors works.

Properties of the cross product

  • Anti-commutative: a × b = −(b × a). Swapping the order flips the direction (right-hand rule).
  • Distributive: a × (b + c) = (a × b) + (a × c), and (a + b) × c = (a × c) + (b × c).
  • Scalar factor: (ka) × b = a × (kb) = k(a × b).
  • Zero self-product: a × a = 0 — and any cross product of parallel vectors is the zero vector.
  • Zero-product meaning: for nonzero vectors, a × b = 0 ⟺ a and b are parallel (mirror of the dot product's perpendicularity test).
Key term — Unit-vector table: i × j = k, j × k = i, k × i = j (cycling forward i→j→k→i). Any reversed pair gives the negative: j × i = −k, k × j = −i, i × k = −j. And i × i = j × j = k × k = 0.

Example 1 — Find the area of the parallelogram with adjacent sides a = (1, 5, −1) and b = (−3, 1, 2).

From Lesson 7.4, a × b = (11, 1, 16). Area = |a × b| = √(11² + 1² + 16²) = √(121 + 1 + 256) = √378 ≈ 19.44 square units. The triangle with the same sides has area √378/2 ≈ 9.72 square units.

Example 2 — Find the area of the triangle with vertices P(1, 0, 2), Q(3, 1, 5), and R(2, 4, 1).

Build two edge vectors from P: PQ = (2, 1, 3) and PR = (1, 4, −1). Their cross product: x: (1)(−1) − (3)(4) = −1 − 12 = −13; y: (3)(1) − (2)(−1) = 3 + 2 = 5; z: (2)(4) − (1)(1) = 8 − 1 = 7. So PQ × PR = (−13, 5, 7), with magnitude √(169 + 25 + 49) = √243 ≈ 15.588. Triangle area = √243/2 ≈ 7.79 square units.

Example 3 — Simplify (a + b) × (a − b) using the cross-product properties.

Distribute: (a + b) × (a − b) = (a × a) − (a × b) + (b × a) − (b × b). Since a × a = 0 and b × b = 0, and b × a = −(a × b), this becomes 0 − (a × b) − (a × b) − 0 = −2(a × b).

✅ Check Your Understanding

Try these on paper first, then reveal. Modelled on §7.7 area problems.

Question 1 — For u = (2, −1, 3) and v = (0, 4, −2), find the area of the parallelogram with sides u and v.

u × v = (−10, 4, 8) (Lesson 7.4). Area = |u × v| = √180 = 6√5 ≈ 13.42 square units.

Question 2 — What is the area of the triangle with the same two sides?

3√5 ≈ 6.71 square units — half the parallelogram's area.

Question 3 — Simplify (2i + 3j) × (i − j) using the unit-vector table.

Distribute: 2(i×i) − 2(i×j) + 3(j×i) − 3(j×j) = 0 − 2k + 3(−k) − 0 = −5k.

Question 4 — Nonzero vectors p and q satisfy p × q = 0. What does that tell you about p and q?

They are parallel — a zero cross product (for nonzero vectors) means sinθ = 0, i.e. θ = 0° or 180°.

📝 Lesson Summary

Key ideas

  • Parallelogram area = |a × b|; triangle area = |a × b|/2.
  • Properties: anti-commutative, distributive, scalar factor, a × a = 0.
  • Unit-vector table: i × j = k, j × k = i, k × i = j (reverse the order → negate).

Need to know

  • Zero cross product ⟺ parallel vectors (the mirror of the dot product's perpendicularity test).
  • For a triangle, build two edge vectors from one vertex before crossing.
