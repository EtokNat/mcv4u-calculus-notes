UNIT 7 OVERVIEW

Dot Product and Cross Product

Textbook: Nelson Calculus and Vectors 12, Chapter 7 §§7.3–7.7 (pp. 350–424). Strand C — Geometry and Algebra of Vectors. Suggested pacing: 10–12 periods.

Unit 7 is about multiplying vectors — and there are two completely different ways to do it. The dot product turns two vectors into a single number: it finds angles, tests for perpendicularity, splits vectors into projections, and computes work. The cross product turns two vectors into a new vector at right angles to both: it finds areas, torques, and normal vectors. By the end you will know which product a problem needs — and why.

Ontario curriculum expectations (Chapter 7)

  • Perform the operation of the dot product on two vectors (7.3, 7.4).
  • Determine properties of the dot product (7.3, 7.4).
  • Determine the scalar and vector projections of a vector (7.5).
  • Perform the operation of cross product on two algebraic vectors in three-dimensional space (7.6).
  • Determine properties of the cross product (7.6).
  • Solve problems involving the dot product and cross product (7.7).

🎯 Learning Goals

  • I can compute a·b = |a||b|cosθ and a·b = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ + a₃b₃, and explain why both are true.
  • I can find the angle between two vectors and classify it as acute, right, or obtuse.
  • I can use a·b = 0 as the perpendicularity test and a × b = 0 as the parallel test.
  • I can find scalar and vector projections and split a vector into parallel and perpendicular parts.
  • I can compute a × b = |a||b|sinθ·n̂ with the right-hand rule, and from components in R³.
  • I can find areas of parallelograms and triangles from |a × b|, and compute work (F·d) and torque (r × F).
