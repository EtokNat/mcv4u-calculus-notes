## choice (3.0 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
A 300 N box sits on a 30-degree ramp. The coefficient of static friction is 0.5. What happens?
```

**Choices:**
- It slides - the downslope component (150 N) exceeds the maximum friction (about 130 N)
- It tips over the edge
- It stays - friction always matches the downslope pull
- It stays - the normal force holds it in place

## essay (16.0 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
A uniform 3.0 m long wooden plank (mass 40 kg) rests horizontally on a pivot located 1.0 m from the plank's left end. A 60 kg child sits on the left end of the plank. (a) Where must a 45 kg child sit on the right side of the pivot so that the plank balances horizontally? Show all steps of your torque calculations. (b) Compute the upward force the pivot must exert on the plank, and explain, in full sentences, why this force must equal the total weight acting on the plank. (c) Explain, in full sentences, why balancing the torques about the pivot is sufficient to guarantee that the system is in equilibrium — that is, what second equilibrium condition must also hold, and how is it satisfied here? (Use g = 9.8 m/s².)
```
