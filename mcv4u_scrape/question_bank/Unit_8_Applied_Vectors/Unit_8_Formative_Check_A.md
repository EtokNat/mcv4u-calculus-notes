## choice (13.0 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
Three cables pull on a pylon with 4 kN, 7 kN, and 12 kN. Can the pylon be in equilibrium?
```

**Choices:**
- Yes — because 12 is the largest
- Only if the 12 kN cable is horizontal
- No — 4 + 7 < 12 violates the triangle inequality
- Yes — three forces always balance
