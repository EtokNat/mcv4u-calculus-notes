## choice (12.0 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
Why does r = r0 + sa + tb need the vectors a and b to be NONcollinear?
```

**Choices:**
- A plane needs three direction vectors
- If a and b were collinear, sa + tb would only trace a line, not a plane
- The parameters s and t must be perpendicular
- The cross product a x b would be too large
