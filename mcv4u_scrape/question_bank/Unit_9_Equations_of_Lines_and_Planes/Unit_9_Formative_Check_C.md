## choice (13.0 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
The line of intersection of p1: x + y + z = 1 and p2: 2x + 2y + 2z = 5 is...
```

**Choices:**

- A single point
- The two planes are coincident
- x = 1 - t, y = t, z = 0
