# Unit 5 Formative Check A

**Unit:** Unit 5 — Derivatives of Exponential and Trigonometric Functions


---

## numeric (12.5 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
[A] A certain radioactive material decays exponentially. The percent, P, of the material left after t years is given by P(t) = 100(1.2)^(-t). Determine the half-life of the substance, in years, to two decimal places.
```

## rich-fill-blank (12.5 pts)

**Prompt:**

```
[A] The number of bacteria in a culture, N, at time t is given by N(t) = 2000[30 + te^(-t/20)]. a. When is the rate of change of the number of bacteria equal to zero? t = b. If the bacterial culture is placed in a colony of mice, the number of mice that become infected, M, is related to the number of bacteria present by the equation M(t) = (N + 1000)^(1/3). After 10 days, how many mice are infected per day?
```
